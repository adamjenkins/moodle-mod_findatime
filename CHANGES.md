# Release notes

## 0.1.0 (2026-09-28)

First release.

- Availability grid with click-and-drag, touch and keyboard input, saved automatically.
- Group overlap heatmap with names on hover and focus, and the best meeting times.
- Confirming, moving and cancelling a group's meeting, with a group calendar event and notifications.
- Optional automatic confirmation of the most popular time, and "any group member may confirm".
- Teacher overview of response rates and meeting status.
- Times stored in UTC and shown in each user's timezone; the activity's times are defined in its own timezone.
- Activity completion, course reset, backup and restore, privacy API, events.
- Supports Moodle 4.5 to 5.3.
